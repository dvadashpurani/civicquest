# Child privacy and content rules (mandatory)

## Data
- Store only a student’s first name, last initial, grade, class, and avatar. No birthdays, addresses, student emails for under-13s, or photos of children.
- Photo uploads show places or work, never people. The upload screen says: “Take a photo of the place, not of people.”
- Never store a student’s location. Strip GPS and all EXIF data from photos before upload.
- Learners and Helpers never get their own accounts. They use class mode on a teacher-signed-in device.
- Leaderboards are visible only inside the school. No public profiles, no chat, and no messaging between students.
- Learners never see individual rankings.
- When unsure whether to collect a piece of student data, don’t.

## Story and mission content
- Use only original characters, like Pip the fox. Never use copyrighted or trademarked characters, brands, or logos.
- Stories for grades 2–5 show near misses and feelings, never injuries or anything frightening.
- Case studies for grades 6–12 are realistic but not about real, named people.
- All civics content is non-partisan: no candidates, parties, or positions on political issues.
