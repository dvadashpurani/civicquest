C. Street signs My pick

Built from the language of real streets: sign panels, highway lettering, and a roadmap drawn as a transit line.

Best for: a look nobody else will have, tied directly to the civic theme. Every kid already knows these shapes from walking to school, so the badges teach real sign meanings too. Overpass is based on US highway sign lettering, and Public Sans was made for government websites. Risk: needs restraint so it doesn’t feel like a traffic manual.

Whichever you pick, the class town stays the memorable element, every screen works in light and dark mode, and text stays large and readable for young students.
